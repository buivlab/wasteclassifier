    Classifier configuration

    EcoVision MobileNetV3-Large (MIT licence, by AmadFR) trained on about 20,000 images: battery, biological, cardboard, clothes, glass, metal, paper, plastic, shoes, trash. 8.4 MB download.

    MobileNetV2 (0.5 width, 160×160) trained on TrashNet: cardboard, glass, metal, paper, plastic, trash. 1.4 MB and about 5× less computation. Needs one item on a plain background; tends to over-predict cardboard/paper.

      Accepts TensorFlow.js layers or graph models, including a Teachable Machine “TensorFlow.js” export. The files stay on this device; they are not uploaded to any server.
